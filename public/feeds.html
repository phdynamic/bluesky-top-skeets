How Top Skeets works

Create your feed

Enter your Bluesky handle and an app password (create one at Settings → App Passwords — it's never stored). Accounts on any AT Protocol server work, not just bsky.social. Pick Top Skeets (your posts ranked by likes) or My Skeets (newest first), optionally give the feed a custom name, description, and icon (any image — it's cropped square automatically), and hit Generate. Checking Include replies creates a separate feed — you can have up to four (each type, with and without replies).

Share it

Copy your feed link and paste it into your Bluesky bio or anywhere else. Your posts load on our server, not in your browser — a progress bar shows how it's going, but you can close the page anytime and the feed will finish populating on its own.

Updates are automatic

New posts show up on their own: within about 15 minutes for My Skeets and about an hour for Top Skeets. Like-count rankings refresh about once a day.

Rename anytime

Re-submit the form with the same account and a new feed name or description. Your feed link never changes, so nobody loses it. The same works for the icon: upload a new image to replace it, or leave it empty to keep the current one.

Unpublish

Enter your credentials, select the feed type (and the replies checkbox, if it's a replies feed), and click Unpublish — it's also available right after creating a feed. This removes that feed from Bluesky and deletes it from our system.
