How saved games work

What is kept, for how long, and how to get yourself or a post taken out.

What a saved game is

The Quote Post Game Tracer can save a quote post game: the original post and everything that quoted it, as it looked on the day. A saved game has its own link and can be refreshed later to pick up new quotes. It is reachable only by its link. There is no list of games, no search across games, and search engines are asked not to index them.

What is not stored

  • Images, avatars, video, link previews or any other media.
  • Likes, reposts, replies, follower lists, or who liked or reposted anything.
  • Anything that was not visible to a logged-out Bluesky viewer. Posts hidden from logged-out viewers, blocked, or labeled adult or graphic are left out (an adult or graphic label means no text is kept).
  • Who created or refreshed a game, anyone's email address, or anyone's internet address. Limits on how often someone may save or refresh are kept in memory only, under a scrambled value, and forgotten within the hour.

When a post is deleted

If someone deletes a post on Bluesky, its text is wiped from every version of every saved game. The card stays in place as "This post was deleted" so replies below it stay attached. This happens when a game is refreshed, when anyone presses "Check for deleted posts", and in a slow background check of every game roughly once a week. A post is only wiped after it has been missing on two checks at least a day apart, so a Bluesky hiccup cannot wipe anything by mistake.

Taking yourself out

The simplest way is to delete the post on Bluesky; it will be wiped as described above. If you want a post or your whole presence removed without waiting, or you want to be kept out of future saved games, send a removal request using the Report link on any saved game (choose "Remove my post") or write to the address below. Removal requests are handled by hand: the account is wiped from the game, or from every game, and kept out of future ones. Signing in with Bluesky to do this yourself is planned but not available yet.

Reporting a game or a card

Every saved game has a Report link, and every card has a Report button. Reasons include personal information, harassment, content that should be labeled, wrong or misleading, and removal requests. A report stores only the reason, your note, the game and the card. It never stores who you are.
