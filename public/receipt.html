What this asks for

  • Sign-in: none. No password, no permissions.
  • Reads: public posts only, the same as someone who isn't logged in.
  • Stored: nothing. Your browser fetches the post and draws the receipt here.

Posts hidden from logged-out viewers, deleted posts, and labeled posts are handled with care. Labeled posts print stats only.

How Skeet Receipt works

  1. 1Paste a link. Copy it from the share menu on any Bluesky post.
  2. 2Print it. Pick whether the post text and handle show up.
  3. 3Share it. Save the image, or copy the alt text for your own post.
