Signing in isn't set up here

This site isn't taking sign-ins right now. To have a post removed, use the Report button on a saved game or write to the takedown address on the policy page.

Sign in with Bluesky

Accounts on any AT Protocol server work, including Blacksky, Northsky and custom domains.

What this asks for

Permission to confirm which account is yours (the base atproto scope, nothing more). It cannot post, follow, read your messages, or see anything private. We use it only to let you remove your own posts from saved games, or to control a game you started. As soon as your account is confirmed the temporary access is thrown away; all we keep for an hour is your account ID, in memory only. No password is typed here.

Games you appear in

Removing wipes your posts to a "removed by its author" placeholder in the game (replies stay attached). It can't be undone here. If you want back in, ask through a report.

Games you started

Freezing stops anyone refreshing a game and keeps the versions that exist; you can unfreeze it. Deleting removes every version for good.
