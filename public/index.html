How Top Skeets works

Create your feed

Enter your Bluesky handle and an app password (create one at Settings → App Passwords — it's never stored). Pick Top Skeets (your posts ranked by likes) or My Skeets (newest first), optionally give the feed a custom name, and hit Generate.

Share it

Copy your feed link and paste it into your Bluesky bio or anywhere else. Your posts load in the background — a progress bar shows how it's going.

Updates are automatic

New posts show up on their own: within about 15 minutes for My Skeets and about an hour for Top Skeets. Like-count rankings refresh about once a day.

Rename anytime

Re-submit the form with the same account and a new feed name. Your feed link never changes, so nobody loses it.

Unpublish

Enter your credentials, choose the feed type, and click Unpublish — it's also available right after creating a feed. This removes the feed from Bluesky and deletes it from our system.
