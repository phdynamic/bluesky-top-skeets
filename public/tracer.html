How the Tracer works

The idea

In a quote post game, people quote the quote, which quotes the quote, and so on, until it is a huge tree. Paste the post that started it and watch the whole tree unfold, then explore it.

How to use it

  1. Paste the link to the post that started the game (the original, not a quote of it) and press Trace.
  2. Watch it grow. The tree fills in as quotes are found, most-quoted branches first. You can browse while it loads, or press Stop.
  3. Explore. Search names and text, fold branches open and shut, change how deep it opens, and trace any quote back up to the original.

Good to know

  • Everything is fetched live from Bluesky by your browser. Nothing is sent to or saved on our server.
  • A trace loads a couple of thousand quotes at a time. Press "Load more" to keep going, up to 20,000 in one trace. If Bluesky slows down, the Tracer waits and picks up again, and you can resume if it stops.
  • Some quotes can't be seen without logging in (locked, blocked or deleted posts). The Tracer tells you how many are missing, and anything quoted under them is missing too.
  • Links on cards open the post on Bluesky.
